📜 HOW TO PLAY
1
EXPLORE THE ISLAND:

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move your character around the island.

2
INTERACT WITH RESIDENTS:

Press E or SPACE near NPCs, mysterious terminals, or island landmarks to talk and inspect.

3
SOLVE CYBER CHALLENGES:

Tackle encoding, cryptography, password deductions, and network reconnaissance to earn boat components.

4
REPAIR YOUR BOAT & ESCAPE:

Collect all 6 boat parts (Hull, Rudder, Engine, Mast, Compass, Sail) from the island residents and return to the beach to escape!

🏝️ THE AEGIS MYSTERY

You arrive on an abandoned island after intercepting a mysterious distress signal from Aegis Sector 4.

The Aegis research facility was supposedly shut down seven years ago—yet evidence suggests parts of the system are still broadcasting.

Investigate the island, solve cybersecurity challenges, and discover the truth about Dr. Mira Sen, Aegis, and ECHO.
